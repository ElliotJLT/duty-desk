    Built to support the people who make your trips.

    Your team and local partners make the trip. The desk keeps track, and people make every decision.

    1. Nothing reaches a guest without your say-so.

      Every guest message and every change to a trip waits for a named person on your team. As it proves itself on routine messages, you can let it send those on its own, one type at a time. You choose, and you can switch it back.

      In the demo: Hannah approves the trip leader's 14:00 start before anything moves.

    2. It only marks a guest as sorted when someone who knows says so.

      That's the guest, your local partner naming them, or the trip leader who's with them.

      In the demo: when a local partner replies "all sorted", it thanks them and asks which guests that covers. And when a guest's flight changes, any draft that no longer fits is withdrawn before anyone can send it.

    3. A quiet guest gets followed up, not forgotten.

      Every guest has someone looking after them and a time to check back. If that passes, it goes to your head of operations with suggested next steps, and the list stays open until the guest is with their group.

      In the demo: Ruth goes quiet. At midday Thursday it brings Hannah her emergency contact, the local partner and the insurer's line. She turns up on Friday, safe, and stays on the list until she's with her group.

    Everything is written down as it happens: who knew what, when, and who decided. That's the record your safety lead, insurer or board will ask for.

    Questions

    Does this replace our ops team or local partners?

    No. It takes the chasing and list-keeping off their plate. Decisions, relationships and the trip itself stay with people.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Nothing reaches a guest without a person approving it, and everything is logged, so any mistake can be traced and put right. Your team stays responsible for decisions, as now.

    Do we have to change anything?

    No. It works from your booking system, pre-trip form or a spreadsheet. Your team sees it in Slack, Teams or email, and partners and guests stay on WhatsApp and email.

    What data does it need?

    For a first trial: guests' first names, flights, trip dates and emergency contacts. Which AI provider is used, where data is stored and how long it's kept are agreed in writing first.
