    What you'd get

    A Slack app

    Each disruption gets its own channel. The desk posts what changed and who it affects, chases the replies, and asks the right person to approve. It's what you just watched.

    A WhatsApp and email bridge

    Guests, local agents and trip leaders reply where they already are. Nobody outside your team installs anything.

    One list

    Who's still unaccounted for, in Slack and on a plain web page for whoever has the duty phone tonight.

    Your AI, if you use one

    Optional: connect ChatGPT or Claude so you can ask "who on the Riviera trip isn't confirmed?". The desk runs its own AI either way, so you don't need to.

    It reads guests' flights from your booking system, your pre-trip form or a spreadsheet, and replaces nothing. Early operators get it run with them by hand while it's built.

    Before

    Watches the flights your guests gave you, including the messy ones: "landing Tuesday morning on the easyJet".

    During

    Tells your local agent and trip leader, and waits for a name back. "All sorted" doesn't count.

    After

    Keeps the record of who knew what and when, for your safety lead, your insurer or your board.
