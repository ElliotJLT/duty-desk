    Built to be trusted with your guests.

    The desk does the chasing. People make every call that matters.

    1. It never acts on its own.

      Every message to a guest and every change to a trip waits for a named person to approve it.

      In the demo: Hannah approves the 14:00 start before anything moves.

    2. It never ticks anyone off on a maybe.

      A guest is confirmed only by someone who can know: the guest, the local agent by name, the trip leader in person.

      In the demo: "All sorted 👍" from the local agent confirms nobody.

    3. Nobody falls off the list.

      Every guest has an owner and a deadline. Missed deadlines go to the duty director, and the incident can't close while anyone is unaccounted for.

      In the demo: Ruth goes to Hannah at midday Thursday, and the room won't close on Friday.

    Everything is written down as it happens: who knew what, when, and who decided. That's the record your safety lead, insurer or board will ask for. It lives in your Slack, and local agents and guests stay on WhatsApp.

    Questions operators ask

    What if the AI gets something wrong?

    It can't send a message to a guest or change a trip without a named person approving it. It only marks a guest confirmed when the right person names them, and everything it does is logged, so a mistake is visible and reversible.

    Do our local agents and guests need to install anything?

    No. They reply on WhatsApp or email, as they do now. Only your team uses Slack.

    Do we have to change our booking system?

    No. It works from whatever holds your guests' flights: your booking system, your pre-trip form or a spreadsheet. It replaces nothing.

    We already send flight alerts. How is this different?

    Alerts tell the guest. The desk closes the loop with everyone else: the local agent moves the pickup, the trip leader knows who's late, and one list shows who's still unaccounted for until each guest is with the group.

    What guest data would you need?

    For a first trial, first names, flights and trip dates. We agree in writing how long it's kept before anything is shared.

    Can I buy it today?

    Not yet. It's early, and the first operators get it run with them by hand while it's built. That's the point: it gets built around how you actually work.
